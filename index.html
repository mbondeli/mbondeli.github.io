Welcome to my website! I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the Institute for the Social Sciences at Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin. I received my Ph.D. from Yale University in May 2025 with Departmental and University Distinction, and was a Harry Frank Guggenheim Emerging Scholar in 2024–25.

In my research, I examine what makes democracies resilient in the face of domestic and international challenges. My dissertation and the associated book project focus on how democracies can counter threats from violent, extremist actors.

My work is generously supported by the Harry Frank Guggenheim Foundation, the National Science Foundation, the American Political Science Association, the Rapoport Family Foundation, and the Yale MacMillan Center.

You can find my publications, working papers, and teaching in my CV.
